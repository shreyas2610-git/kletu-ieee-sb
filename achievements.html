IEEE KLETU Student Branch · Recognition

A record of achievement

Milestones earned by the branch, its members, and the community we've built since 2009 — across awards, events, research, and membership.

#

What the branch has earned

Each entry below represents a real milestone — replace with your branch's actual data as it grows.

  1. 01

    Avinya Shishir 2025 — recognition

    Recognized among the top-performing IEEE Student Branches at Avinya Shishir 2025, the annual IEEE Bangalore Section student symposium, for outstanding event management and membership engagement throughout the academic year.

    2025
  2. 02

    Computer Society chapter approved

    The IEEE Computer Society Student Branch Chapter received formal approval on 04 December 2026, making it the branch's first fully ratified society chapter and opening access to CS-specific resources, conferences, and funding.

    2026
  3. 03

    IEEEXtreme — national standing

    KLETU teams representing the branch achieved a competitive national ranking in IEEEXtreme, the 24-hour global programming competition, competing against teams from institutions across India and 60+ countries.

    2024
  4. 04

    150+ active members

    The branch crossed 150 paid IEEE members in a single academic year — one of the stronger membership counts in the North Karnataka subsection — reflecting sustained outreach, chapter programming, and peer referrals.

    2024
  5. 05

    20+ events in one academic year

    Organized more than 20 technical and professional development events in 2023–24 — workshops, hackathons, paper presentation contests, industry visits, and career seminars — setting a new branch record.

    2024
  6. 06

    IEEE Xplore publications

    Research papers authored by branch members were published on IEEE Xplore following presentations at IEEE-sponsored conferences, establishing a research culture at the branch and creating a visible track record for future students.

    2023
  7. 07

    WIE affinity group petitioned

    Submitted and progressed the petition for a Women in Engineering Affinity Group under the branch, creating a dedicated platform for gender inclusivity in STEM at KLE Technological University.

    2023
  8. 08

    IEEE Distinguished Speaker Program

    Hosted IEEE Distinguished Speaker Program sessions featuring researchers from industry and academia, drawing attendance from students across Hubballi and providing exposure to globally recognized technical expertise.

    2022

Fifteen years of progress

Selected milestones from the branch's journey, from a founding cohort to a multi-chapter community.

  1. 2009

    Branch founded

    IEEE Student Branch established at KLE Technological University with an initial cohort of students and the support of the IEEE Bangalore Section.

  2. 2013

    First society chapter

    Launched the first society-level chapter, expanding technical programming and giving students access to discipline-specific IEEE resources.

  3. 2018

    IEEEXtreme participation begins

    First KLETU team registers for IEEEXtreme, starting a tradition of competitive programming participation that continues each year.

  4. 2020

    Virtual pivot during the pandemic

    Pivoted to online events, conducting 10+ webinars and virtual workshops in 2020 alone, maintaining community momentum through disrupted semesters.

  5. 2023

    Research publications on IEEE Xplore

    Branch members publish research papers through IEEE conferences, establishing the branch as a research-active student community.

  6. 2025

    Avinya Shishir recognition

    Recognized at the IEEE Bangalore Section's annual student symposium for outstanding branch activities, capping the strongest academic year in branch history.
