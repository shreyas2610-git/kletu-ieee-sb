What is an affinity group?

IEEE affinity groups are formal communities within a student branch that gather members around a shared characteristic — professional, personal, or interest-based. They operate alongside technical chapters and carry their own programming, leadership, and IEEE recognition.

How to join

Membership in any affinity group at KLETU is open to all IEEE student members, regardless of gender, discipline, or year. Reach out to the group's liaison or watch the branch's social media for membership drives and onboarding sessions.

WIE

Women in Engineering

IEEE Women in Engineering is one of the largest international professional organisations dedicated to promoting women engineers and scientists, and inspiring girls around the world to follow their academic interests into a career in engineering. The WIE affinity group at KLETU creates a peer network, mentoring opportunities, and events that celebrate and support women in technical disciplines — while remaining welcoming to all members who share these values.
