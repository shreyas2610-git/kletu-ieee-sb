When Monday, 27 July 2026
Where SME Seminar Hall
Hosted by Student Branch

Girl Geeks 2026 Workshop-1

Girl Geeks 2026 Workshop-1 marked the commencement of the Girl Geeks 2026 programme at KLE Technological University, Hubballi. The workshop introduced participants to the programme's objectives, challenge statements, emerging technologies, and the complete product development lifecycle, laying a strong foundation for developing innovative solutions to real-world challenges.

Organizers

  • KLE Tech IEEE Student Branch (STB62711)

  • KLETECH IEEE Computer Society Student Branch Chapter (SBC62711C)

  • KLETECH IEEE Women in Engineering Student Branch Affinity Group (SBA62711)

Collaborating Organizations

  • IEEE Bangalore Section

  • IEEE Computer Society Bangalore Chapter

  • IEEE Women in Engineering Bangalore Section

  • IEEE Sensors Council Bangalore Chapter

  • KLE Technological University, Hubballi

Speakers

  1. Dr. R. M. Banakar – Professor, Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering

  2. Mr. Prashant Narayankar – Faculty Advisor, KLETECH IEEE Computer Society Student Branch Chapter (SBC62711C)

Topics Covered

  1. Artificial Intelligence (AI)

  2. Machine Learning (ML)

  3. Internet of Things (IoT)

  4. Voice AI and Natural Language Processing (NLP)

  5. Front-End Development

  6. Dashboards and Data Visualization

  7. Product Development Lifecycle

  8. Ideation and Prototype Development

? Why This Workshop Matters

The workshop provided participants with a comprehensive understanding of modern technologies and practical product development methodologies required for hackathons and innovation-driven projects. Through expert guidance, students learned how to transform ideas into functional prototypes while gaining exposure to industry-relevant technologies and development practices.
