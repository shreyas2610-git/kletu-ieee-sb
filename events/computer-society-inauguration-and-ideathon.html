Computer Society Inauguration & Ideathon

The official inauguration of the IEEE Computer Society Student Branch Chapter at KLE Technological University, coupled with a high-impact Industry Day and collaborative Ideathon competition.

When Saturday, 23 May 2026
Where Department of E&C, KLE Tech
Hosted by Computer Society

About the Event

The IEEE KLE Tech Student Branch, in collaboration with the IEEE Bangalore Section and associated chapters, organized the Computer Society Chapter Inauguration and a subsequent Industry Day & Ideathon. The primary objective of the session was to expose students to current industry trends and emerging technologies through expert sessions delivered by seasoned technology professionals, while bridging the gap between academic learning and real-world industrial expectations.

Distinguished Speakers & Judges

The event featured expert technical sessions and a project evaluation panel comprising highly accomplished specialists from industry and research:

  • Mr. Alok Ranjan — Chair-Elect of the IEEE Computer Society Bangalore Chapter and Group Leader for Advanced Tech & Innovation at Bosch Global Software Technologies, Bengaluru. He shared insights on Embedded Systems, Edge AI, and building robust industry-academia innovation networks.

  • Mr. Chidambar Yadappanavar — Scientist F and Deputy Project Director of IVV Communication Systems at the Aeronautical Development Agency (ADA), Bengaluru, who mentored students on large-scale project execution and structural testing.

  • Mr. Satish Tembad — Co-Founder of Explicate Inc. and CTO of CyberHuman.ai, with over 18 years of technology leadership spanning signal processing, handheld systems, and internet-of-things (IoT) ecosystems.

The Ideathon Competition

Prior to the presentation day, teams submitted project proposals in a screening round. Out of over 50 submissions, the top 10 teams were shortlisted to pitch their ideas live. Each team was allotted 4 minutes for their slide presentation and 1 minute for a rigorous Q&A session with the judging panel.

The evaluation criteria focused on innovation, feasibility, technical implementation, relevance, and overall potential impact. The participants displayed excellent creativity and deep technical knowledge, pitching cutting-edge concepts such as a Piezoelectric-Based Energy Generation System designed to harness electricity from mechanical pressure and environmental vibrations.

"Success is not just about winning; it's about creating impact, inspiring innovation, and empowering future leaders."

Key Highlights & Outcomes

  • Over 10 Ideas Shortlisted & Pitched: The rigorous screening process successfully evaluated over 50 submissions down to 10 final presentation pitches.

  • Top 3 Winning Teams Recognized: Selected by the expert judges, the top 3 projects were awarded prizes and are being encouraged to develop their concepts further towards prototype stage.

  • Expert Guidance: Direct Q&A sessions allowed students to clarify career-related and technical queries with leaders from Bosch, ADA, and Explicate.

  • Academic Integration: Students learned to translate concepts from text-books into industry-relevant solutions, fostering critical thinking, teamwork, and entrepreneurial mindsets.
