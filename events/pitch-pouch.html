Pitch Pouch

The IUCEE Student Chapter at KLE Technological University proudly hosted Pitch Pouch, a sustainability-focused event where creativity met entrepreneurship!

When Monday, 27 July 2026
Where SME Seminar Hall
Hosted by Computer Society

🌱 Pitch Pouch — Craft. Pitch. Convince.

The IUCEE Student Chapter at KLE Technological University proudly hosted Pitch Pouch, a sustainability-focused event where creativity met entrepreneurship!

Participants were challenged to transform eco-friendly materials — think newspapers and craft paper — into functional, reusable pouches and shopping bags. But the challenge didn't stop at design. Teams then stepped into the shoes of entrepreneurs, delivering persuasive sales pitches to convince our judges of their product's real market potential.

From durability and design to practical viability as a genuine alternative to single-use plastics, every team showed that sustainable innovation doesn't have to compromise on functionality or flair. ♻️

This event reflects what our IUCEE Student Chapter stands for:

🤝 Building a collaborative, peer-driven engineering community — bringing students together to exchange ideas and grow beyond the classroom
🔧 Bridging academic learning with practical experience — turning theoretical knowledge into hands-on, real-world problem-solving
💡 Promoting innovation — nurturing creativity and solution-oriented thinking through student-driven challenges
🌍 Encouraging engineering for societal impact — inspiring purpose-driven solutions to real environmental challenges

A huge thank you to all the participants who brought their creativity, business acumen, and sustainable mindset to the table, and to everyone who helped organize and judge this event! 🙌

As part of the Indo Universal Collaboration for Engineering Education (IUCEE), our chapter continues to work toward our mission of empowering engineers for a global future — one innovative, hands-on initiative at a time.

Stay tuned for more from the IUCEE Student Chapter, KLE Technological University! 🚀
