01
CASS

Circuits & Systems Society

The Circuits and Systems Society focuses on the theory, analysis, design, and practical implementation of circuits, and the application of circuit theoretic techniques. The KLETU CASS chapter connects students with design methodologies for analog, digital, and mixed-signal circuits, sensor interfaces, and neuromorphic processing systems.

  • Analog circuits
  • Digital systems
  • Signal processing
  • Neuromorphic systems
Abbreviation IEEE CASS
Membership Open to all students

02
CEDA

Council on Electronic Design Automation

IEEE CEDA focuses on the advancement of computer-aided design (CAD) algorithms, system-level design methodologies, and hardware-software co-design. The KLETU CEDA chapter provides students with opportunities to engage in VLSI design, logic synthesis, hardware security, and electronic design automation tools that power next-generation silicon chips.

  • VLSI systems
  • CAD algorithms
  • Hardware security
  • SoC design
Abbreviation IEEE CEDA
Membership Open to all students
View office bearers
03
PES

Power & Energy Society

The Power & Energy Society is the leading provider of scientific and engineering information on electric power and energy. The KLETU PES chapter introduces students to the challenges of generating, transmitting, and distributing electrical power. Members focus on grid modernization, renewable energy integration, energy storage systems, and sustainable practices.

  • Power systems
  • Grid integration
  • Energy storage
  • Sustainability
Abbreviation IEEE PES
Membership Open to all students
View office bearers
04
CS

Computer Society

The oldest and largest technical society in IEEE, the Computer Society chapter at KLETU brings together students interested in computing across all its dimensions — from software engineering and machine learning to computer architecture and cybersecurity. Members gain access to IEEE Xplore's computing literature, participate in coding challenges and project sprints, and connect with the global CS community.

  • Software engineering
  • Machine learning
  • Cybersecurity
  • Computer architecture
Abbreviation IEEE CS
Membership Open to all students
View office bearers
05
PELS

Power Electronics Society

The IEEE Power Electronics Society chapter facilitates the development of power electronics technology, which is critical to modern power conversion systems. At KLETU, the chapter connects students with the systems that control and convert electrical power, spanning EV drivetrains, solar inverters, and high-efficiency converters, while promoting research in energy conservation.

  • Power converters
  • Renewable energy
  • EV systems
  • Smart grids
Abbreviation IEEE PELS
Membership Open to all students
View office bearers
06
RAS

Robotics & Automation Society

The Robotics and Automation Society chapter provides a structured platform for students to explore the interdisciplinary field of robotics. Integrating mechanical design, electronic control systems, embedded systems, and machine learning, members work on building autonomous systems, motion planning, and intelligent automation for real-world applications.

  • Autonomous systems
  • Control systems
  • Embedded platforms
  • Computer vision
Abbreviation IEEE RAS
Membership Open to all students
