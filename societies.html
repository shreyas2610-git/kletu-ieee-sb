Computer Society

The oldest and largest technical society in IEEE, the Computer Society chapter at KLETU brings together students interested in computing across all its dimensions — from software engineering and machine learning to computer architecture and cybersecurity. Members gain access to IEEE Xplore's computing literature, participate in coding challenges and project sprints, and connect with the global CS community.

  • Software engineering
  • Machine learning
  • Cybersecurity
  • Computer architecture
Status Chapter approved
Abbreviation IEEE CS
Membership Open to all students

Power Electronics Society

Power electronics sits at the heart of every modern energy system — from EV drivetrains and solar inverters to industrial motor drives. The PES chapter connects students with the engineering discipline that converts and controls electrical power. Members engage with design challenges in converters, renewable energy integration, and smart grid technologies, supported by IEEE PES's extensive publication library and global conference network.

  • Power converters
  • Renewable energy
  • EV systems
  • Smart grids
Status Petition endorsed
Abbreviation IEEE PES
Membership Open to all students

RAS Petition in approval

Robotics & Automation Society

Robotics and automation are reshaping manufacturing, healthcare, logistics, and exploration. The RAS chapter gives students a structured entry point into this interdisciplinary field — combining mechanical design, control systems, embedded programming, and AI. From building autonomous robots to studying manipulation algorithms, members work on problems that span the full stack of a robotic system.

  • Autonomous systems
  • Control systems
  • Embedded systems
  • Motion planning
Status Petition endorsed
Abbreviation IEEE RAS
Membership Open to all students
